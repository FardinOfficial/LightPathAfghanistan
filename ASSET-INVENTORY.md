`community-study-session.webp`, `science-workshop.webp`,
`teacher-training-session.webp`, `library-bookshelves.jpg`,
`library-opening-banner.jpg`, `books-and-materials.jpg`,
`ribbon-community-gathering.jpg`, `courtyard-architecture.jpg`) and the generic
upload filenames (`01.jpeg`–`05.jpeg`, the WhatsApp image) were removed after the
new approved photographs were supplied. The legacy library banner contained an
obsolete email address and is no longer published.

No AI-generated photographs, invented testimonials, fake statistics or stock
imagery presented as Light Path's work are used.
